## 1. The world

**Vesalius = dental. Mundi = world.** The series title is a plain statement: the dental world.

**Vesalius Mundi is the whole oral environment** — not a row of teeth, not a neighbourhood of
molars. Tom's page draws it as one large circle, and the anatomy written around the rim is the
world's landscape:

Soft tissue · Blood Vessels · Lips · Gingiva · Tongue · Nerve · Ligament · Cheek ·
Periodontal · Hard tissue · Pulp · Bone · Cementum · Dentin · Enamel · Saliva Glands ·
Taste Buds

**OS — the mouth — is the Portal to Total System Access.** Inside the circle sits a
mouth-shaped form, labelled `OS` at both ends with `(MOUTH)` beneath, and inside it the words
`PORTAL TO TOTAL SYSTEM ACCESS`. Beneath the form: `RATIO TOTALIS ACCESSUS`.

That is the scale of the argument: the mouth is the way in to the whole system. Any episode
that treats Vesalius Mundi as a set of teeth has shrunk the world.

**The open door of the world:** `"All who have mouths have a Place Here."` Written on the
page, in the hero block. It is the world's invitation and its inclusiveness — everyone with a
mouth is in this story.

## 2. The hero

**Aurea Puella** — `(Golden Girl)`. Written on the page under the name.

Her titles, as written:
- `PROTECTOR OF VESALIUS MUNDI`
- `LEADER OF THE PREVENTION`
- `Super Hero!`

Her charge, as written: *"Able to repair and restore to last a lifetime those teeth that Fall
prey to the Villains of Vesalius Mundi."*

**Aureal is her short form** (Tom's ruling, 11/20). Not a rename — the five character images
already filed stay filed under Aureal.

She is the protector and the leader of prevention. Both halves matter: she repairs, and she
prevents. Prevention is the lead position, not the consolation prize.

## 3. The Order of Oralis — the villains

On the page, a red-outlined jagged shape is labelled `the Order of Oralis` and `Bacteria`,
joined by a large handwritten `OR`. **The Order of Oralis is the bacteria.** There is no
separate organisation behind them.

**Led by TOOB** — `"LED By"` points at `TOOB`, drawn as an orange-and-red block.

Under TOOB, the Order's rank and file, as written on the page:
- **Mutans Streptococci** (a small black stick-figure creature is drawn beside this name)
- **Sobrinus** — presumably *Streptococcus sobrinus*, written as Tom spells it. **Unconfirmed.**
- **Lactobacillus**

These three are the only named members of the Order. The villains of Vesalius Mundi are
bacteria; they are not insurers, manufacturers, schools or dentists.

> **Conflict on file, needs Tom:** the approved message spine carries a "no named villains"
> rule. That rule was written to keep real-world targets off screen. Tom's page names bacteria
> as villains, which is a different thing — but the spine rule as written does not carve it
> out. Do not treat the bacteria as cleared villains until Tom confirms.

## 5. The Dental Team

Listed on the page as `DENTAL TEAM`, then four material abbreviations:

- **Zr** — zirconia
- **LiSi** — lithium disilicate
- **PFM** — porcelain fused to metal
- **COMPO** — composite

**COMPO is a team member** (Tom's ruling, 11/20). Composite belongs on the dental team, as a
legitimate defender — beside Zr, LiSi and PFM, exactly where the page puts it. COMPO is not a
villain, not a victim, and not a failed material.

**And in the pilot scene, Compo IS a tired old filling** that has been in the patient's mouth
seven or more years. Both statements are true and must hold together: he is a team member and
a veteran past his best. Write him as a professional who has served a long time — never as a
loser, never as a punchline, never as proof that composite is bad.

**The defence routine**, written as a vertical list on the page:

- BRUSH 1 AM
- BRUSH 2 PM
- FLOSS DAILY
- CHECK-UP
- CLEANING
- HYDRATE
- 7PH

**7PH is a character: sePHen.** The letters **pH** are the joke, and 7 is neutral pH (Tom's
ruling: sePHen is one of the characters on the team). The name is written `sePHen` — lower-case
se, capital PH, lower-case en. Keep that casing.

Below `7PH` on the page, the routine's supplies: **Fluoride · Toothpaste · Sealants.**

## 9. What this means for anything we write

- The world is the whole oral environment, entered through the mouth — not a row of teeth.
- The villains are bacteria. The dental team is materials, routine, and the people in the
  Human Link. Nothing on the page makes a person or an institution a villain.
- Seven defenders exist; **their names are not settled.**
- COMPO is a defender who is also, in the pilot, a tired old filling. Hold both.
- Concede front teeth to ceramics; the argument is the back of the mouth. (Standing rule,
  spine §3.2 — not on the page, but not contradicted by it.)
- No episode may put a number on screen that did not arrive from `spine-sources.md`.
