## The split

**EGC 30% · Lauren 35% · Alex 35%**

## Of what

Gross amounts actually paid out by the social platforms for Versalius Mundi content —
TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook creator and ad-revenue programmes.

**Explicitly not included:** GDN signups, EGC product sales, merchandise, licensing,
sponsorship or brand deals. Any of those entering the split requires a written amendment
signed by all three parties.

## What EGC's 30% is for

Production costs and advertising for Versalius Mundi. It is a working budget, not profit
EGC withdraws. The better the content performs, the larger every share grows — EGC's
return on this project is not the 30%.

## Ownership of the work

EGC owns the name **Versalius Mundi**, the characters (including Aureal), the world bible,
the scripts, the artwork and all footage produced for the series.

Lauren and Alex hold a **revenue interest, not an ownership interest**. If the series is
ever licensed, optioned or sold, their share follows the receipts the series generates;
ownership of the property stays with EGC.

## Vesting — *Tom's call, recommendation below*

Recommended: each share vests across the first **24 months of active work**, with a
**3-month cliff** (nothing vests before month 3). Following through is then literally what
pays them, and a partner who drifts in month four does not carry a share for a decade.

## If one of them leaves — *Tom's call, recommendation below*

Recommended: the vested share continues on content produced while they were active, and
stops earning on content produced after they leave. Unvested share is forfeited.

## If production costs exceed EGC's 30% — *Tom's call*

Recommended: EGC covers the shortfall and the overage is recovered from future receipts
before any distribution.

## Reporting

A monthly statement of platform receipts, shared with Lauren and Alex within 15 days of
month end.

## Term

Reviewed annually. Any change in writing, signed by all three parties.
